Taking care of your mind doesn't require big changes. Small, consistent habits
make the biggest difference over time. Here are five you can start today.

## 1. Start your day without your phone

Give yourself the first 15 minutes of the day before checking notifications.
This lowers morning anxiety and helps you set your own tone for the day.

## 2. Get sunlight and movement

A 10-minute walk outside boosts mood, regulates sleep, and reduces stress
hormones. It doesn't need to be exercise — just movement and daylight.

## 3. Write down three good things

Each evening, note three things that went well. Gratitude journaling is one of
the most researched and effective mood-lifting habits.

## 4. Practice one mindful minute

Pause once a day for sixty seconds of slow breathing. In through the nose for
four counts, out for six. This calms the nervous system almost immediately.

## 5. Connect with one person

A short message, call, or conversation counts. Social connection is one of the
strongest protective factors for mental wellbeing.

---

*Start with just one habit this week. Small steps, repeated daily, become
life-changing routines.*
