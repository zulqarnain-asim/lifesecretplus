Don’t fear failure as it often the road of success. some of the greatest success stories in life are people who failed multiple times before becoming successful. if they had given up the first time after failing, then they never would have become highly successful.

Many of us are afraid of failing, at least some of the time. But fear of failure (also called “atychiphobia”) is when we allow that that fear to stop us doing the things that can move us forward to achieve our goals.

Fear of failure can be linked to many causes. For instance, having critical or unsupportive parents is a cause for some people. Because they were routinely humiliated in childhood,they carry those negative feelings into adulthood.

It is easy to find successful people who have experienced failure.For example:

Micheal Jordan is widely considered to be one of the greatest basketball player of all times. He was cut from his high school basketball team because his coach didn’t think he had skill.
