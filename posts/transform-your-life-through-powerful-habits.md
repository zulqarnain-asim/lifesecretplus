A habit is something that you do often and regularly, sometimes without knowing that you are doing it. It is a routine of behavior that tend to occur subconsciously. Behavior is something greater than knowledge, because in life there are many situations where knowledge fails but behavior can handle everything.

A habit is an action you do often and subconsciously in response to your environment.

Five Facts About Habits.

40% OF OUR DAILY BEHAVIORS ARE THE RESULT OF HABITS.

85% OF REGULAR EXERCISERS REPORT HAVING A SET TIME/PLACE TO EXERCISE.

40% OF LONG -TERM HABIT GOALS ARE GIVEN UP WITHIN THE FIRST THREE WEEKS.

TIME REQUIRED TO FORM A HABIT, MIN 9 TO 18 DAYS, MED 60 TO 72 DAYS, AND MAX 180 TO 265 DAYS.

40% OF people who has a desire to eat healthier, exercise more and save more money for comfortably living.
