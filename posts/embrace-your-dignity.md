Self respect is a sense of honor and dignity about yourself, your choices, and your life. It is knowing that not everyone will treat you well and choosing to respect everyone, but understanding that you deserve to surrounded by great people. Respecting yourself is important to keep a positive self-image by allowing yourself to feel confident in who you are and happy with the person you are becoming.

Value your own opinion of yourself. People will try to make you feel bad about yourself, they will judge you your appearance or how you walk. The fact of the matter is your own opinion. Most of the time, people are saying is not about you, it’s about themselves feel good by putting you down.

For example, if someone makes a comment about you wearing makeup or bright green t-shirt, don’t let you feel down. Just respond in “cool way, I beg you pardon you don’t like it, but I love wearing makeup and this green. It makes me happy to wear it.

Self respect is having the courage to stand up for yourself when you are being mistreated than what you deserve. It is all about being the kind of person that pleases others you care about. It reflect on your life that the things you have done and feel a great deal of self- respect. If you are not proud of who you are then you could be compromising your own values. Don’t lower your standards for anyone or anything. SELF RESPECT IS EVERYTHING.
